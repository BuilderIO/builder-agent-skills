# Filler Phrases to Cut

Remove these on sight. Replace with the fact itself or delete entirely.

## Throat-clearing openers

- "In today's world..."
- "It's worth noting that..."
- "It's important to understand..."
- "When it comes to..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- "Let's dive in..."
- "Here's the thing..."
- "Here's what you need to know..."
- "The reality is..."
- "Simply put..."
- "To put it simply..."

## Emphasis crutches

- "Truly"
- "Really"
- "Very"
- "Extremely"
- "Incredibly"
- "Absolutely"
- "Fundamentally"
- "Essentially"
- "Basically"
- "Actually"
- "Literally"
- "Clearly"
- "Obviously"
- "Undoubtedly"

## Hedge and softening

- "It seems that..."
- "It appears that..."
- "One might argue..."
- "It could be said..."
- "In many ways..."
- "To some extent..."
- "More or less..."
- "Kind of"
- "Sort of"
- "Perhaps"
- "Maybe"
- "Potentially"

## Announcement phrases

- "This article will explore..."
- "We'll walk through..."
- "In this post, we'll cover..."
- "The goal of this piece is..."
- "What follows is..."

## Empty transitions

- "That said..."
- "With that in mind..."
- "Moving on..."
- "On the other hand..." (when used as filler, not contrast)
- "Furthermore..."
- "Moreover..."
- "Additionally..." (when the next sentence already follows logically)

## Significance inflation

- "Game-changer"
- "Revolutionary"
- "Transformative"
- "Cutting-edge"
- "Next-level"
- "Paradigm shift"
- "Landscape" (as in "the AI landscape")
- "Unlock" (as in "unlock potential")
- "Leverage" (when "use" works)
- "Empower"
- "Seamless"
- "Robust"
- "Holistic"

## Adverbs

Cut adverbs unless they change meaning in a way no other word can. Most `-ly` words are cuttable.
